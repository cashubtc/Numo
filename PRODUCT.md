# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Merchants using an Android point-of-sale terminal to enter charges, select catalog items,
and receive Cashu or Lightning payments from customers.

## Product Purpose

Make checkout, saved baskets, payment history, and mint management understandable and
usable on a handheld Android device. The any-unit work extends these existing workflows.

## Brand Personality

Preserve Numo's existing identity and Material 3 controls, semantic colors, typography,
and light/dark themes. This pass improves consistency rather than defining a new brand.

## Anti-references

Avoid controls that diverge from neighboring screens, invisible dialogs, clipped labels,
crowded content, and ornamental motion that interferes with checkout.

## Design Principles

- Keep the amount, unit, and issuing mint clear wherever they affect a payment.
- Reuse the app's established components and semantic resource tokens.
- Keep actions and selections visible, with consistent spacing and feedback.
- Preserve the merchant's data and choices while reviewing the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native Android accessibility semantics, readable light/dark contrast, adequate touch
targets, and layouts that accommodate larger text and long unit or mint names.
